# Research agenda

## Priority 0 — audit and close the split-prime theorem

**Goal:** turn the current giant-fiber formula into a theorem with no hidden symmetry or chart assumptions.

Deliverables:

- explicit Descartes Gram matrix and characteristic exclusions;
- split/nonsplit criterion;
- dual-quadric derivation of tangent parameters;
- arbitrary-anchor affine count;
- exact boundary-at-infinity count;
- independent exact-enumeration regression.

A successful result should be phrased as a theorem over all odd primes outside an explicitly listed exceptional set.

## Priority 1 — complete the nonsplit case

Classify the same pencil on (Q^-(3,p)).  Determine whether exceptional parameters produce tangent one-point projective sections or another affine anomaly, and give a uniform formula across both Witt classes.

**Field contribution:** a complete local classification for the Descartes pencil, rather than a split-only phenomenon.

## Priority 2 — lift from (p) to (p^k)

Count normalized solutions modulo (p^k) to
[
Q(x)equiv0,qquad B(x,a)equiv c x_0pmod{p^k}.
]

Separate regular from singular/tangent fibers.  Seek:

- Hensel-stable formulas for regular fibers;
- singular lifting trees for exceptional fibers;
- limiting (p)-adic local densities.

**Field contribution:** inputs suitable for singular series and higher-level congruence diagnostics.

## Priority 3 — joint fibers and correlation geometry

For anchors (a,a'), classify
[
Q(b)=0,quad B(a,b)=c,quad B(a',b)=c'.
]

Projectively this is line–quadric intersection.  Build a complete incidence taxonomy and exact counts, then extend to triple constraints where feasible.

**Field contribution:** pair-correlation/local pattern data for thin-orbit and packing statistics.

## Priority 4 — ambient quadric versus Apollonian orbit

For primes where congruence images are understood:

1. compute the exact ambient fiber law;
2. enumerate the actual Apollonian orbit mod (p);
3. run generating-set random walks;
4. measure convergence of fiber statistics;
5. isolate bad-prime or orbit-specific deviations.

Do the same mod (p^k) once Priority 2 is available.

**Field contribution:** an explicit diagnostic separating geometric irregularity from thin-group irregularity.

## Priority 5 — reciprocity-sensitive diagnostics

Use the modern failure of the naïve local-global conjecture as motivation to test whether reciprocity obstructions correlate with higher-level fiber statistics, orbit components, or character twists invisible in mod-(p) ambient geometry.

A meaningful negative result is also valuable: showing that giant-fiber statistics are purely geometric and blind to reciprocity would cleanly delimit the method.

## Priority 6 — higher-dimensional Descartes/Soddy-Gossett systems

Replace the surface by higher-dimensional isotropic quadrics associated with sphere-packings.  Classify tangent-hyperplane strata, exact finite-field counts, and any analogues of heavy fibers.

**Field contribution:** a uniform local-geometric theory for exceptional sections in arithmetic packing groups.

## Suggested theorem sequence

- **T1:** complete split/nonsplit mod-(p) classification.
- **T2:** exact regular-fiber mod-(p^k) lifting theorem.
- **T3:** singular-fiber mod-(p^k) classification.
- **T4:** exact joint two-functional counts.
- **T5:** orbit-vs-ambient equidistribution theorem/quantitative diagnostic under a stated spectral-gap hypothesis.
