# J1 regime ledger

Generated by `tools/make_regime_ledger.py` from `reference/parameter_corpus.py`.
Do not edit; `make_regime_ledger.py --check` fails the build on a hand edit.

Two kinds of column appear below and they carry different authority.

- **Verified** columns are recomputed from the parameter by exact arithmetic
  on every render: the cycle witness, its multiplier class, and the
  critical-orbit verdict. A row cannot claim one of these and be wrong.
- **Tag** columns name an imported classical theorem. Each one was judged by
  the literature gate `docs/literature-gate-2026-09-19.md`, which located a
  source, recorded the qualification that is now part of the tag's meaning,
  and says what it did not do -- no theorem number was checked against a
  printed page. A gate verdict is not a proof, and no verified column below
  depends on a tag.

`J1 expects` says what the separator catalogue must do at this parameter if
`J1` is sound. It is the point of the corpus: the `STABILIZES` rows are the
confirmation cases that the parameter plane cannot supply. `UNDECIDED` records
an unresolved J1 obligation, not permanent undecidability or a claim that
local connectivity fails. The algebraic-multiplier import and its analytic
boundary are recorded in the notes rather than in the local-connectivity tag
column.

`CAPPED` is not a classification. Squaring doubles the coordinate bit length,
so an exactly iterated critical orbit that neither escapes nor repeats costs
`O(2^n)` bits at step `n`; every parabolic and irrationally indifferent row
exhausts its budget by construction, and reports which budget ran out.

## Notes

- **`c0`** (superattracting fixed) -- J_0 is the quadrance-1 locus. It is not a circle at rank 2, and no arc, angle, or circumference is available from it.
- **`basilica`** (period-2 centre) -- Attracting-cycle certificate with a superattracting witness; the interior is the pullback of the immediate basin.
- **`dendrite`** (Misiurewicz dendrite) -- Postcritically finite with empty interior: the IN certificate has nothing to certify, so the classifier is OUT-or-UNKNOWN here.
- **`dendrite_conjugate`** (Misiurewicz dendrite, conjugate) -- The fifth and last postcritically finite parameter of Q(i), by section 3.3 of docs/tiling-connections-2026-09-20.md. It is the complex conjugate of the dendrite and dynamically conjugate to it, so it adds no regime; what it adds is that the corpus now holds every such parameter rather than four of the five.
- **`chebyshev`** (Chebyshev endpoint) -- Degenerate real case: the exclusion machinery must handle a Julia set carried by one rational segment.
- **`cauliflower`** (parabolic, multiplier 1) -- Fail-closed case: the multiplier has quadrance exactly 1, so an interval test that decides attracting or repelling here is unsound.
- **`parabolic_minus_one`** (parabolic, multiplier -1) -- Root of the period-2 component: the same fail-closed obligation with a negative multiplier.
- **`parabolic_i`** (parabolic, multiplier i) -- The third of the four Gaussian-rational roots of unity, reached by c = lam (2 - lam) / 4 at lam = i.
- **`niven`** (irrationally indifferent) -- The exact algebraic multiplier lam = (3 + 4i)/5 is not a root of unity. The literature import in docs/literature-gate-2026-10-06-algebraic-multiplier.md gives Diophantine and Brjuno; the accepted linearization and Herman imports give a Siegel disk with the critical point on its boundary. Local connectivity remains OPEN, and J1 remains UNDECIDED. These are classical imports, not new finite-core verdicts.
- **`escaping`** (escaping critical orbit) -- K_c is disconnected, and the imported fiber theory is stated for compact connected full sets, so the question the other rows answer is not posed here; the escape certificate is.
