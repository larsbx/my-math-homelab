# Paper Outline — Current State

## Working title

**Rank-One Quadratic Images of Product-One Moduli: Nielsen Tuples, Trace-Gram Invariants, and Finite-Field Arithmetic**

The old title centered on "fiber-of-multiplication closure laws." That language is too broad because the mechanism is established prior art.

## 1. Introduction

- State the existing multiplicative-moduli umbrella.
- Explain the restricted new question: what survives when finite-group factorization data is pushed through quadratic representations into rank-one algebraic groups?
- State exclusions: no analytic angle primitives, no unit-circle ontology, no transcendental parametrization.
