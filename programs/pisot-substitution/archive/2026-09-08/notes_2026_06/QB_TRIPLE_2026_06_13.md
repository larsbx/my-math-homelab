# Q-B: the no-trapped-triple question — surrogate census and verdict (2026-06-13)

## Verdict
Q-B does **not** furnish an over-constraint beyond the pair case. The triple-overlap
surrogate behaves like the pair surrogate in every measured respect: a PF ceiling
PF(N_C^triple) < β with zero violations, the same spectral floor (~0.35, matching the pair
resonance floor 0.3473), and **no closed triple SCC** (the trapped-triple fingerprint)
anywhere in the producing corpus. The triple coupling — that the third offset δ₃−δ₂ is
determined by δ₂, δ₃ — adds no new corpus-visible obstruction. The "no trapped triple"
target is therefore the same wall as G2 / Q-A condition (1): the object is the
counterexample itself, untestable on a counterexample-free corpus.

## The one provable structural fact (manuscript-grade, a priori on the hypothetical triple)
**Triple PF ceiling.** For a triple-overlap component the mass-balance identity
β·w(s) = Σ_children w(c) makes (w(s))_s a positive PF-eigenvector of N_C^triple with
eigenvalue β. By Perron–Frobenius on the (nonnegative) child-count matrix, PF(N_C^triple)
≥ β always, and = β iff the component is closed (no leak to coincidence). A producing
(open) triple component leaks, so PF < β strictly — exactly as in the pair case
(companion Thm 2.2). The trapped-triple fingerprint is PF = β with a closed component;
none exists in the corpus. This is the verbatim analog of the pair ceiling, now proved for
triples via the (gated) triple partition identity. It is a NECESSARY property of the
hypothetical trapped triple, provable a priori; it does not exclude the triple.

## Why Q-B does not over-constrain (the coupling is a bounded symbolic quotient)
The only new content of a triple over three pairs is the coupling δ₃−δ₂ = (δ₃)−(δ₂):
the third avoidance walk is the difference of the other two, not independent. But this
coupling is a **bounded symbolic function** of the pair coordinates — exactly the kind of
quotient the no-coarse-quotient meta-theorem says cannot reflect the avoidance target.
The triple avoidance walk in Z^{2k} projects to three coupled Z^k avoidance walks; the
coupling is linear and bounded, so it carries no information the positional residue does
not already carry. Hence Q-B inherits the G2 wall rather than escaping it. This is
consistent with the meta-theorem and explains the surrogate census: no new spectral
feature appears because none can appear at the count/marginal level.
