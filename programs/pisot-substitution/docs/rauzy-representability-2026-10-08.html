How much of the closed PSC work is a statement about Rauzy fractals?

About a quarter. Of the 47 closed, repository-proved claims, 11 are natively Rauzy statements and 2 more are partly so. The remaining 34 either use invariants that a Rauzy fractal cannot see or stay on ZA without reaching the projection.

The split matters more than the count. The Rauzy side carries the live route to pure discrete spectrum and the open frontier. The non-Rauzy side mostly carries the alternative G1 + SCC Producer route.

Dividing line

Rauzy fractals see only abelian, degree-1 data

A Rauzy fractal is the closure of the contracting (or adelic) projections of the Parikh vectors of prefixes. Whatever it records has already passed through the abelianisation map ℓ.

X = closure{ π_c(ℓ(p)) : p a prefix of u = σ^ω(a) }, ℓ : A* → Z^A R ⇔ the claim's content factors through π_c ∘ ℓ (the internal-space image) R ⇒ it factors through ℓ (necessary, not sufficient)

The ¬R block uses degree-≥2 scattered-subword and free-Lie invariants: K₂, wedge spans, mid-area and mean-area, the orientation cocycle, and degree-4 defects. These are non-abelian, so ℓ erases them. They are absent from every Rauzy fractal by construction.

Factoring through ℓ is necessary but not enough. A claim that stays on ZA itself, such as the intertwiner P N = M P or a rational-rank bound, is abelian but says nothing about the projected pieces, so it is ¬R. It is R⁻ when part of its content does pass to the projection.

Where the Rauzy side sits

Caveats

Reading the number correctly

  • “Rauzy” means adelic. The standing regime includes non-unit PIP. Use K∞ × ∏p | det M Kp (Siegel; Minervino–Thuswaldner). A Euclidean fractal only works when |det M| = 1. See proof-ladder.md rule 5.
  • Representable is not novel. The repository records Rauzy-language ancestors for several R claims: Siegel–Thuswaldner's G(0), Arnoux–Ito strong coincidence, compactness of the fractal, Barge–Gambaudo. What is new is the non-unit transfer, the possibly illegal swap seeds, and the PDS ⇒ G1 package.
  • Not counted. The base set is repository-proved claims only. The box-automaton note's finite-domain consequence is ledgered as BoundedPureDiscreteSpectrum (status finite-domain), so it falls outside that class; its universal Lemma Ω1 and Theorem Ω, the leftmost-chain proposition (2026-10-05) and the vertex-coincidence box note (marked not yet reviewed) are proved in their notes but are not ledger claims. All of them are native R. Update 2026-10-08 (PR #241): Lemma Ω1, Theorem Ω, Proposition LC and Corollary LC5 are now repository-proved ledger nodes (BoxCycleContainment, BoxAutomatonCertificate, LeftmostChainCycleStructure, LeftmostChainG1Certificate); the counts on this page are as of its baseline and do not include them.
