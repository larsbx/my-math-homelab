
using it as immovable foundation for further high-level deductions. A review
should explicitly walk the realization step, fibre statement, coincidence-rank
implication, and the passage from PPVC to all-seed G1/termination.

### P1 — keep the shortest route at seed strength

Issue #84 needs only one productive periodic swap seed. Issue #138 asks for the
strictly stronger statement that every distinct letter pair is eventually
coincident for the substitution and its reversal.

A useful intermediate target is therefore:

> **Bi-good seed-pair target.** For every ternary PIP substitution, there is an
> unordered pair `{a,b}` that is eventually coincident for both `sigma` and
> the reversed substitution.

This is not asserted here as a theorem. It is a research target closer to the
actual hypothesis of Theorem 5.38 than universal all-pairs strong coincidence.
If established, it removes the aligned obstruction for a chosen swap seed while
leaving only the strict interior zipper branch.

### P2 — the strict-zipper route has reached the geometry/coverage boundary

Issue #139 has successfully retired several insufficient mechanisms:

- bare affine-cycle exclusion;
- fixed-radius collars;
- generic Perron growth;
- Euclidean-only stable projection;
- deeper finite-cokernel / M-adic quotient filtering as an independent route;
- one-step contracting-size descent.

The exact carry theorem is especially useful: after descaling, deeper
finite-cokernel filtering recovers the same occurrence-labelled affine update
already present in the overlap graph. Another quotient-depth sieve therefore
adds no new universal mechanism by itself.

**Action:** focus the next theorem attempt on an occurrence-compatible
**adelic coverage / recurrence / alignment lemma** for the graph-directed
prefix-difference object. A useful statement must add information not already
encoded by the affine graph: self-affine coverage, recurrence of realizable
ordered prefixes, recognizability alignment, or a comparable full-internal-space
mechanism.

### P2 — use C4 as a portable theorem laboratory

Issue #9 has produced a strong exact reduction stack: the Parikh intertwiner,
orientation cocycle, signed defect intertwiners, free-Lie spectral sieve,
hierarchy-offset state, endpoint/hub reduction, and now realizable target-aware
packets.

The October 2 target-aware diagnostic is informative precisely because target-
free recurrent packet SCCs can exist while successful target paths also exist.
Existential accessibility is too weak. The good-edge bridge further isolates
the uncontrolled datum as the **ordered hub-residual word on interior
children**.

**Action:** continue C4 only with lemmas designed to transfer to the finite
seed-patch overlap setting of #84/#139. Unless a C4 lemma transfers across that
boundary, it remains a secondary G1-conditional route rather than the shortest
PSC route.

### P2 — preserve the evidence boundary on PPVC censuses

PeriodicPairVertexCoincidence is an excellent falsification framework: for a
fixed substitution, Proposition V reduces the all-period statement to a finite
exact graph check, so a failure would give a finite certificate against PSC for
that substitution.

The standing 4,554-specimen row is CI-pinned. The larger total-image-length
`<= 8` and image-length `<= 4` rows are recorded exact runs rather than full
CI obligations.

**Action:** keep those larger runs as finite-domain evidence unless and until
their complete execution is guarded. Do not use them as a substitute for a
uniform theorem.
