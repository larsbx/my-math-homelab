## Theorem R: return module

Audited source: [fibre literature gate, §5](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/blob/347c0d9fb20cd3a7339c053f02ce1db4c386cf48/docs/p1b-periodic-pair-fibre-literature-gate-2026-10-02.md).

The character argument is valid. A character of Z^A into Q/Z that vanishes
on tile-return vectors makes prefix characters depend only on the letter
at the endpoint. This produces the edge coboundary H(c) − H(a) and the
function K(a) = H(a) + chi(e_a). Counting along a legal sequence gives an
integer linear relation between the letter frequencies for every value h.
Rational independence forces the indicator functions of H and K to agree,
so chi vanishes on every basis vector. A proper subgroup of Z^A has a
nonzero character into Q/Z because its finitely generated quotient has a
nonzero cyclic quotient. Thus the return module is Z^A.

The PIP specialization also checks: irreducibility in characteristic zero
gives distinct conjugate eigenvalues; the conjugate Perron eigenvectors
form a basis, so a rational relation among their coordinates is zero. The
argument concerns uncollared tile returns, Lambda_1. It does not assert that
every collared patch-return lattice Lambda_n is full, and the nonunimodular
patch-return examples on main therefore do not invalidate it.

## Proposition V: completeness and the converse

Audited source: [vertex-coincidence box note](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/blob/347c0d9fb20cd3a7339c053f02ce1db4c386cf48/docs/p1b-vertex-coincidence-box-2026-10-02.md)
and [exact certificate](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/blob/347c0d9fb20cd3a7339c053f02ce1db4c386cf48/mojo/psc/vertex_coincidence.mojo).

For a periodic overlap, the contracting-coordinate recurrence has a
periodic solution bounded by C_k/(1 − mu_k). The physical overlap bounds
the expanding coordinate. Pairing these bounds with the trace-dual basis
gives the stated integer-coordinate radii. Descendants of the finite
start set remain bounded in every embedding. The Minkowski image of
Z<ell> is a lattice, so this closure is finite.

The converse is essential and valid: vertices without an offset-zero
descendant form a child-closed set. A terminal strongly connected component
has branching in the graph with child occurrences counted. Mixing a
non-leftmost edge with a non-rightmost edge produces a cycle whose centre
is interior. Its composed prefixes yield genuine interior occurrences,
and (M^r − I) is invertible because none of its eigenvalues is 1. Theorem B
then constructs legal periodic tilings with no common vertex. This step
does not assume that the initial overlap came from a pair of legal tilings.

The implementation matches that argument. Algebraic upper bounds use exact
root sign tests; the complex modulus test uses N(x)/x with the sign of x
handled explicitly. The inverse trace form gives the dual lengths. Solving
one coordinate against the physical slab enumerates a finite superset of
the required box. The exact child closure and reverse reachability decide
offset-zero descendants. Treating tile coincidences as absorbing success
does not affect the verdict or the reported noncoincidence recurrence.
Incomplete closures return capped and never become successful certificates.

## Theorem S and the fibre interface

Audited sources: [Proposition F](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/blob/347c0d9fb20cd3a7339c053f02ce1db4c386cf48/docs/p1b-periodic-pair-fibre-literature-gate-2026-10-02.md),
[Theorem B](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/blob/347c0d9fb20cd3a7339c053f02ce1db4c386cf48/docs/p1b-strict-zipper-periodic-pair-2026-10-02.md),
and [Theorem S](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/blob/347c0d9fb20cd3a7339c053f02ce1db4c386cf48/docs/p1b-vertex-coincidence-box-2026-10-02.md).

Proposition F extends Solomyak's eigenvalue criterion from tile returns to
Z<ell> using Theorem R. The Pisot convergence rate makes the supertile-phase
limit uniform over the finite prefix alphabet. Evaluating that limit at
the two integral centre offsets gives equal eigenfunction values and hence
the same maximal-equicontinuous-factor fibre. No unimodularity assumption
is inserted into this step.

Barge's coincidence-rank results apply to the legal Pisot tiling space:
two same-fibre tilings sharing no vertex share no tile and force coincidence
rank at least two. Pure discrete spectrum has coincidence rank one.
Consequently PDS implies PPVC. Conversely, PPVC rules out a reachable
child-closed strict-zipper set through Lemma C and Theorem B. Manuscript
Proposition 5.47 then bounds the gaps between common vertices and yields
finite swap-seed BPA, the specified G1. Neither reversed implication nor
strictness is established by this audit.
