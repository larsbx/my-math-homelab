# Padovan / Plastic-A post-proof conjecture program — 2026-09-23

**Branch:** `research/post-proof-padovan-plastic-a`  
**Status:** research note / conjecture program. **Not a proof source and not a promotion of the live PSC claim boundary.**

This note records the structural conjectures suggested by the Plastic-A stress-test computations and the associated Padovan recurrence. It is deliberately downstream of the finite-witness proof architecture and must not be read as replacing the repository's live roadmap or claim-status surfaces.

## 1. Verified computational findings motivating the program

The current experiments support the following finite-domain facts for the benchmark specimens already studied.

### Plastic-A

Use

```text
1 -> 2, 2 -> 3, 3 -> 12.
```

On the canonical deep chain:

- coincidence horizon is 12 base-substitution steps;
- the exact span law is `span(k) = beta^(k+3)`, where `beta` is the plastic number;
- the chain word lengths follow the Padovan recurrence;
- the apparent threshold discrepancy `n_0 = 15` versus horizon `H = 12` is a seed-normalization offset, `n_0 = H + 3`;
- the radius-0 FM witness is nontrivial mod 2 at every tested transition of the depth-12 chain;
- the radius-1 centered multiset profile separates every tested state;
- the pair-symbol witness admits the exact compression `M_0^pair ->> M_0^FM`, because the diagonal coordinates are determined by the off-diagonal counts together with the Parikh vector.

### Iterate-family interpretation

The depth-12, depth-6, and depth-4 tiers seen in the survey are generated by one iterate family, not by independent arithmetic families. If `sigma` is Plastic-A, then the visible depth of `sigma^n` is governed by the base horizon 12 and the iterate scale.

### Padovan role

Padovan is the **quantitative skeleton** of the Plastic-A benchmark:

- word lengths,
- geometric spans,
- threshold offsets,
- and horizon timing

are controlled by the same recurrence / `Z[beta]` arithmetic.

Padovan is **not** a logical ingredient of the finite-witness proof architecture.

## 2. Main conjectures

### Conjecture P1 — Plastic-A extremality

Among root three-letter primitive irreducible Pisot substitutions in the intended survey domain, Plastic-A realizes the maximal coincidence horizon.

**Test:** exhaust the root-specimen quotient by relabeling and iteration and compare exact horizons.

### Conjecture P2 — Iterate-family exhaustion above depth four

Every three-letter coincidence depth `> 4` arises, up to relabeling, from the Plastic-A iterate family.

**Test:** canonicalize all deep specimens by root substitution and iterate exponent.

### Conjecture P3 — Threshold / horizon alignment

For canonical deep chains there is a seed-scale offset `s_0` such that

```text
n_0 = H + s_0,
```

where `n_0` is the first supertile scale dominating the finite discrepancy and `H` is the first coincidence horizon.

Plastic-A gives `s_0 = 3`.

**Test:** record exact seed scale, threshold, and horizon on every deep root specimen.

### Conjecture P4 — FM quotient universality

For balanced-pair dynamics, the canonical first radius-0 witness compression is the off-diagonal/FM quotient

```text
M_0^pair ->> M_0^FM,
```

and every nontrivial consecutive noncoincident transport is detected in

```text
M_0^FM / p M_0^FM
```

for some prime `p`.

The diagonal coordinates are algebraically redundant once the Parikh vector is fixed.

**Negative control:** specimen-dependent active/inert quotients are allowed to fail. Exotic-B is a calibration against overstrong active-quotient claims.

### Conjecture P5 — Small-prime detection

There exists a small finite set of primes that detects all radius-0 FM transport in the three-letter benchmark domain. A stronger candidate is that `p = 2` suffices on the deep-chain class.

**Test:** enumerate the least detecting prime for every nontrivial FM transport step.

### Conjecture P6 — Padovan extremal skeleton

Plastic-A is the unique three-letter extremal benchmark whose witness activation timing is governed by the Padovan recurrence, up to relabeling and iteration.

This is a statement about the quantitative benchmark structure, not the proof logic.

### Conjecture P7 — terminal `x^d = x + 1` Pisot branch

The `x^d = x + 1` Pisot branch terminates at the Fibonacci / plastic cases relevant here: the degree-3 plastic case is the terminal higher-alphabet member of that recurrence mechanism.

**Important:** this does not imply that all higher-alphabet extremal behavior disappears; it only says that a continuation of this particular mechanism must come from another family.

### Conjecture P8 — three-letter extremal difficulty

The three-letter plastic case is the hardest alphabet-size benchmark for the current finite-witness mechanism, in the sense of maximizing normalized coincidence horizon among the tested root families.

This is deliberately weaker than the statement "three-letter PSC is globally hardest" and should remain benchmark-level until a uniform higher-alphabet comparison theorem exists.

## 3. The finite-witness / arithmetic bridge

The Plastic-A chain provides a useful model for how exact algebraic arithmetic feeds finite proof objects.

The exact chain lives in `Z[beta]`:

- Parikh vectors evolve by powers of the incidence matrix;
- spans are powers of `beta`;
- word lengths follow Padovan;
- coincidence appears at a finite lattice alignment.

The finite-witness architecture then compresses this into:

```text
Z[beta]-evolution -> finite balanced-pair states -> M_0^FM / p M_0^FM.
```

Research target: make this passage explicit enough to compare recurrence families without making the recurrence family itself proof-load-bearing.

## 4. Immediate computational program

### C1. Root-family canonicalization

For every surveyed specimen:

1. quotient by relabeling;
2. identify whether it is an iterate of a smaller root specimen;
3. record root-family ID and iterate exponent;
4. recompute depth/horizon on the root-normalized scale.

### C2. Horizon atlas

Record for every root specimen:

- coincidence horizon `H`;
- seed-scale offset `s_0`;
- threshold `n_0`;
- Perron root `beta`;
- automaton size;
- SCC size / cycle statistics.

Primary target: test P1-P3.

### C3. FM quotient atlas

For every nontrivial balanced-pair transport step:

- record the 6 off-diagonal coordinates;
- reduce mod `p`;
- find the least detecting prime;
- retain an exact replayable witness.

Primary target: test P4-P5.

### C4. Exotic-B negative control

Use Exotic-B to test the boundary between:

- universal FM compression;
- specimen-dependent active/inert compression.

A successful FM witness together with active-quotient failure is evidence for the hierarchy

```text
M_0^pair ->> M_0^FM ->> M_0^active,
```

with only the first arrow theorem-facing.

### C5. Higher-alphabet family search

Search for degree-4 and higher root specimens that can compete with the Plastic-A normalized horizon.

Do not search only the naive `x^d = x + 1` continuation. Explicitly include Rauzy-type and non-unit families.

Primary target: falsify P8 if possible.

"More specimens passed" is not itself a promotion criterion.
