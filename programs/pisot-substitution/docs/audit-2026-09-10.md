## 2. Scope and notation

`FCS` means that a finite closed nonproductive recurrent noncoincident SCC `C` of the balanced-pair graph is already given.

This distinction matters:

- **G1 is used to pass from a global nonproductive region to such a finite sink SCC.**
- Once an FCS is given, many subsequent structural lemmas do not separately assume global BPA finiteness.
- The global endpoint synchronization theorem does not require Pisot or G1 at all.

So it is too broad to say that all recent work is "inside the G1 box." The accurate statement is:

> The current proof route from failure of C1 to a finite obstruction uses G1 at the sink-SCC extraction step; most C4 structure is then conditional on the resulting finite closed SCC rather than on global BPA finiteness itself.

## 6. G1 and the balanced-pair literature: corrected bridge obligation

Akiyama, Barge, Berthé, Lee, and Siegel, *On the Pisot Substitution Conjecture* (2015), Theorem 5.3, states for an irreducible Pisot substitution that pure discrete symbolic spectrum is equivalent to termination with coincidence of the standard balanced-pair algorithm; it also gives a swap-seed criterion of the form `(ij,ji)`. The survey points to earlier balanced-pair work including Sirvent–Solomyak and Martensen.

This is highly relevant to G1, but it does **not** justify skipping from one convenient seed to the repository's all-seed graph.

The repository uses the finite family of legal swap seeds

```text
(ab,ba),  a != b,
```

modulo side-swap normalization. Its graph is the union of the single-seed irreducible-factor closures.

Therefore the repository bridge needed for a bare theorem

```text
PDS => G1
```

must establish one of the following:

1. PDS implies termination/finiteness for **every legal swap-seed closure** represented in the repository graph; or
2. a separate theorem reduces the full legal swap-seed family to one seed and proves that every other seed closure is contained in or reachable from that terminating closure.

Termination from just one seed, without such a family-reduction theorem, is not enough to prove finiteness of the all-seed union.

`docs/bpa-literature-bridge.md` records the repository union lemma and keeps the remaining seed-family implication explicit. Until it is closed, the safe statement is:

> Literature gives a PDS/termination criterion for the standard balanced-pair algorithm; applicability to the repository's entire normalized legal swap-seed union still requires the seed-family bridge.

## 7. Sieve saturation and strategic pivot

The recent three-state work is useful calibration: it shows exactly which invariants distinguish increasingly realistic golden negatives. It should now stop as a main proof spine.

For a fixed finite BPA, counterexample existence is decidable by building the graph. A new finite sieve has proof value only when accompanied by a **uniform completeness statement** over substitutions or SCC sizes.

The two strongest theorem-grade ingredients not yet applied to the actual factorization obstruction are:

### 7.1 Prefix-difference return structure plus Pisot contraction

For a balanced pair `T=(u,v)`, define

```text
D_T(k)=Parikh(u[:k])-Parikh(v[:k]).
```

Irreducibility means `D_T(k)!=0` at every interior cut. Under substitution, image ordering expands this into a piecewise lattice walk; its returns to zero are exactly the balanced child boundaries.

For a finite closed SCC, state lengths are bounded while iterates grow at rate `beta`, so the inflated pair contains bounded-gap zero returns. The next uniform theorem should couple that return density to contraction in the stable Pisot directions.

### 7.2 Recognizability / supertile-boundary alignment

In the gauge-trivial orientation case, the SCC gives two distinct Parikh-equal word morphisms with a common derived substitution. Existing notes do not prove that balanced-child cuts align with `sigma`-supertile boundaries. Mossé-style recognizability is the natural tool for upgrading recurrent cut patterns into alignment or contradiction.

The next proof target should therefore be an **alignment-or-return-density lemma**, not another `|C|=3` arithmetic sieve.
