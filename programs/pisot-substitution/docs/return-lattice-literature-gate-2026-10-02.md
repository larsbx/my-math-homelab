# Literature gate: return vectors of patches and `Z<ell>` — 2026-10-02

**Status:** literature baseline, stop/go decision and exact finite-domain
census (`kernel/return_lattice_census.mojo`). **Decision: proceed with a
narrowed target** (§7). Identifying the fibre relation of the maximal
equicontinuous factor through `Z<ell>`-valued return vectors is sound in the
unimodular corpus classes and unsound for `|det M| = 2` once patches have
positive radius. Nothing here concerns pure discrete spectrum, G1 or the Pisot
substitution conjecture.

## 1. Exact proposed check

For a primitive irreducible Pisot `sigma` on `{0,1,2}` with Perron tile lengths
`ell`, `p -> <ell, p>` identifies `Z^3` with `Z<ell> = Z ell_0 + Z ell_1 +
Z ell_2`. Let `Lambda_n <= Z^3` be spanned by the Parikh vectors of
displacements between two occurrences of one length-`n` factor. The
literature reads the fibre relation off return vectors; that reading presumes
the return vectors span all of `Z<ell>`. The check: compute
`[Z^3 : Lambda_n]` for every corpus specimen and every `n <= 32`.

## 3. Prior art and terminology

- **Return vectors** in Solomyak and Barge–Gambaudo are displacements between
  translates of one **tile**; that is `Lambda_1` here. Return vectors of
  patches (`Lambda_n`, `n > 1`) are the radius-`n` refinement.
- **The Rauzy-graph route is Barge–Gambaudo Lemma 15 in one dimension.** The
  order-`n` Rauzy graph is a model of the order-`n` collared
  Anderson–Putnam complex, and `l` on its cycle space is the first-letter
  cocycle. `psc/return_lattice.mojo` proves equality of the image with
  `Lambda_n` directly (a character kills `Lambda_n` iff it is a coboundary on
  the graph), so the kernel claims no novelty for the identification.
- **Non-unimodular factor.** Barge–Kellendonk's `lim_←(T^3, hat Lambda^*)` for
  `|det| = 2` is a 2-adic solenoid. A fibre relation stated as "`T' = T - v`
  for `v in Z<ell>`" describes a torus factor and cannot be right there.

## 6. Census and the elementary arguments around it

Exact over the 4554 specimens and `n = 1..32` (`pixi run return-lattice-census`):

```text
specimens by |det M| 1:2628 2:1926
Lambda_1 = Z^3: 4554  of 4554
profiles verified against the chain and the covering bound: 4554
unimodular with Lambda_1 = Z^3, hence Lambda_n = Z^3 for every n: 2628  of 2628
unimodular with an index > 1 at some order: 0
|det M| = 2 still index 1 at order 32: 0
|det M| = 2 by first order with index > 1 2:564 3:528 4:540 5:186 6:84 7:12 13:12
|det M| = 2 by log2 index at order 32 2:288 3:1278 4:348 5:12
sampled cross-checks: 276  equal to exact: 276
```

Three arguments, each elementary, turn this into statements:

1. **Chain.** A return of a length-`n+1` factor returns its length-`n`
   prefix, so `Lambda_{n+1} <= Lambda_n`.
2. **Covering bound.** If `|sigma^K(a)| >= n` for every letter, each
   `sigma^K(a)` contains a length-`n` factor at a fixed offset, so returns of
   `a` map to returns of that factor: `M^K Lambda_1 <= Lambda_n`. With
   `Lambda_1 = Z^3` (all 4554 specimens), unimodularity gives
   `Lambda_n = Z^3` for **every** `n`, and `|det M| = 2` gives an index
   dividing `2^K`.
3. **Unbounded growth for `|det M| = 2`** (imported recognizability). Past the
   level-`k` recognizability radius two occurrences of a factor carry the same
   `sigma^k`-cutting, so `Lambda_n <= M^k Z^3`, of index `2^k`. The census
   sees the growth through `n = 32` only; the argument, resting on Mossé, is
   what makes it unbounded. `cap_n Lambda_n` is an `M`-invariant lattice on
   which `M` acts bijectively; irreducibility and `|det M| = 2` leave only
   `{0}`.

## 7. Decision

**Proceed, narrowed.** The census is an exact finite-domain certificate of
(a) `Lambda_1 = Z^3` on the corpus, (b) `Lambda_n = Z^3` for all `n` on the
unimodular classes, through the covering bound, and (c) the index of
`Lambda_n` exceeding `1` by `n = 13` on every `|det M| = 2` specimen.
Repository work that identifies fibres through `Z<ell>` cosets may cite (b);
for `|det M| = 2` it must use Barge–Kellendonk's direct limit
`E = lim_→(Gamma, Lambda^*)` instead. No novelty is claimed for the lattice
identification (Barge–Gambaudo Lemma 15) or for the solenoid
(Barge–Kellendonk Corollary 5.11).

## 8. What this does not establish

- Nothing about substitutions outside the 4554-specimen corpus.
- The growth of the index past `n = 32` rests on imported recognizability,
  not on this run.
- Mossé's papers and Baker–Barge–Kwapisz beyond its abstract were not re-read
  for this gate.
