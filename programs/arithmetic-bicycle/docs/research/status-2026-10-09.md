# Research status — 2026-10-09

The graph theorem now has a fresh independent audit and four additional
regressions, taking its suite to 21 tests. Its integral proof, original
53-case receipt, and 70 receipt tower pairs remain unchanged.

The next contribution establishes ABC-REGULAR-MODULAR-BICYCLE for a fixed
full-row-rank totally unimodular matrix A and every integer n >= 2.
The map sends A transpose y to [AA transpose y/n]. An integral right
inverse supplies potential uniqueness over composite rings. The proof
establishes representative independence, injectivity, surjectivity,
both inverse identities, specified coordinate equivariance, and both
compatible modulus maps. Its written proof, proof audit, and independent
exact evidence support the fixed-TU claim's `proved` state.

There are 16 new tests, for 37 tests in the combined suite. The TU receipt
covers seven fixtures, 36 modulus cases, 44 proper tower pairs, and 12
separately labeled non-TU control cases. A cographic K3,3 representation
has Smith factors (1,3,3,9). Tests also check all 729 ternary 2-by-3
matrices: the 456 full-row-rank TU examples pass at four moduli.

The nonprimitive matrix A=[2] exposes a false strengthening in the
bootstrap formulation: its bicycle module is trivial modulo 2 and
nontrivial modulo 4, whereas H[2]=0 implies H[4]=0 for every abelian
group H. The unconditional finite-group torsion model is `retracted`,
with its own counterexample proof. This example is torsion-free but not
surjective as an arithmetic matroid. It does not compare two
realizations and therefore does not refute realization invariance.

ABC-MAIN and ABC-REALIZATION-INVARIANCE remain `open`. Regular-matroid
representation independence is the next blocking target. Specified
unimodular coordinate changes are covered by the fixed-TU proof;
the theorem relating all TU realizations of one matroid is stated in
Backman–Baker–Yuen, Section 2.1, Lemma 2.1.1. Its labeled hypotheses and
application to the tower still need an audit and repository proof record.
Future arithmetic work must keep
realization invariance separate from the withdrawn group strengthening.
