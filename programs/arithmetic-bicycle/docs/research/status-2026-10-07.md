## Principal conjecture

For a torsion-free representable arithmetic matroid \(\mathcal M\) and integral realization \(A\), define the modular bicycle module
\[
\operatorname{Bic}_n(A)=\operatorname{im}(A^\top\bmod n)\cap\ker(A\bmod n).
\]

ABC asserts realization invariance of the compatible tower \(\{\operatorname{Bic}_n(A)\}_{n\ge2}\).
