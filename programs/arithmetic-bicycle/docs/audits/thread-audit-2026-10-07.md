## Important corrections retained from the thread

- The principal baseline should be upgraded to an arbitrary-modulus module isomorphism, not merely equality of dimensions.
- Arithmetic-matroid multiplicity is lattice-index data; determinant squares arise naturally in Cauchy-Binet but are not the generic definition of multiplicity.
- Projective geometry is a derived field-valued shadow, not the integral truth layer.
- Regular/TU theory is a baseline theorem zone; arithmetic realization independence is the conjectural jump.
