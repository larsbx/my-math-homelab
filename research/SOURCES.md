# Source Ledger

Primary and high-value secondary references used to set the research boundary.

## Multiplicative moduli and character varieties

- A. Alekseev, A. Malkin, E. Meinrenken, **Lie Group Valued Moment Maps**, J. Differential Geom. 48 (1998), 445–495.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/dg-ga/9707021

- W. Crawley-Boevey, P. Shaw, **Multiplicative preprojective algebras, middle convolution and the Deligne–Simpson problem**.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0404186

- V. P. Kostov, **On the Deligne–Simpson problem**.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0011013

## Hurwitz spaces and Nielsen theory

- M. D. Fried, H. Völklein, **The inverse Galois problem and rational points on moduli spaces**, Math. Ann. 290 (1991), 771–800.  
  https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01459271

- K. Magaard, S. Shpectorov, H. Völklein, **A GAP Package for Braid Orbit Computation and Applications**, Experimental Mathematics 12 (2003), 385–394.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0304376

- J. Ellenberg, A. Venkatesh, C. Westerland, **Homological stability for Hurwitz spaces and the Cohen–Lenstra conjecture over function fields**, Annals of Mathematics 183 (2016).  
  https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2016/183-3/p01

- B. Seguin, **Fields of Definition of Components of Hurwitz Spaces**.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05903

- K. Christ, X. He, I. Tyomkin, **The irreducibility of Hurwitz spaces and Severi varieties on toric surfaces**.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16238

- A. Landesman, I. Levy, **Homological stability for Hurwitz spaces and applications**.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03861

## Citation policy

Use primary sources whenever a theorem is stated. Survey and expository references may orient discussion but should not be the sole support for a main theorem claim.
